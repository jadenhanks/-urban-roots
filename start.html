Get started

Start your own urban garden

You do not need a farm or even a yard. Here is how to grow your first plants in five simple steps.

Step by step

Five steps to your first harvest

  1. Step 1

    Find your space

    Choose a balcony, backyard, windowsill, or another suitable space.

    Watch the spot for a day. Many vegetables need 6 to 8 hours of direct sun, but leafy greens like lettuce can handle some shade.

  2. Step 2

    Choose your plants

    Try beginner-friendly plants such as herbs, lettuce, or tomatoes.

    Start small. Two or three kinds of plants are easier to look after than ten.

  3. Step 3

    Prepare your containers

    Use containers with drainage holes and suitable potting mix.

    Most vegetables need containers at least 6 to 8 inches deep. A tomato needs a big one, about 5 gallons of mix.

  4. Step 4

    Give plants what they need

    Provide appropriate sunlight, water, and care.

    Containers dry out faster than the ground. Water when the top of the soil feels dry, until water runs out of the drainage holes.

  5. Step 5

    Watch your garden grow

    Be patient and enjoy learning how plants develop.

    Keep notes or take a photo each week so you can see how much your plants have grown.

Easy plants for beginners

Match a plant to your space. Sunlight and container size matter most.

Beginner plants with their sunlight and container needs
PlantSunlightContainerWhy it is good for beginners
LettuceSome shade is OKShallow pot, 6 inches or moreGrows fast and you can pick leaves a few at a time.
BasilFull sun, 6 to 8 hoursMedium potPinching the tips makes it bushier, so it keeps giving leaves.
TomatoFull sun, 6 to 8 hoursLarge pot, about 5 gallonsOne plant can keep making fruit all summer.
CarrotSome shade is OKDeep pot with loose mixFun to pull up, and short kinds suit containers.
